Today's Reminder

For today

Every act of worship becomes lighter when done with presence — even a single ayah read slowly, with the heart attending, outweighs pages read in a hurry. Come back to this page when the day feels heavy; it will still be here.

۝

Qur'an

Short ayahs, held for a moment longer than usual.

Surah Ash-Sharh, 94:5–6

فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

For indeed, with hardship comes ease.

Repeated twice in the same passage — not as a promise for later, but a description of what is already happening alongside the difficulty.

Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:286

لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا

Allah does not burden a soul beyond what it can bear.

Worth returning to on the days that feel like too much — the weight was measured before it was given.

Anxiety & Overwhelm

Allah is sufficient for me+
حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.
Refuge from anxiety and grief+
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ O Allah, I seek refuge in You from anxiety and grief, from helplessness and laziness, from cowardice and stinginess, from being overwhelmed by debt and overpowered by others.
The dua of Yunus (AS) in distress+
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been among the wrongdoers.
Not left to myself, even a moment+
اللَّهُمَّ رَحْمَتَكَ أَرْجُو فَلَا تَكِلْنِي إِلَى نَفْسِي طَرْفَةَ عَيْنٍ، وَأَصْلِحْ لِي شَأْنِي كُلَّهُ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ O Allah, it is Your mercy I hope for — do not leave me to myself even for the blink of an eye, and set right all of my affairs. There is none worthy of worship but You.

Sadness & Depression

The Qur'an as the spring of the heart+
اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلِ الْقُرْآنَ رَبِيعَ قَلْبِي، وَنُورَ صَدْرِي، وَجَلَاءَ حُزْنِي، وَذَهَابَ هَمِّي O Allah, make the Qur'an the spring of my heart, the light of my chest, the remover of my sadness, and the reliever of my anxiety.
Gratitude in every circumstance+
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَلَى كُلِّ حَالٍ All praise is due to Allah in every circumstance.
Upon loss or calamity+
إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ Indeed we belong to Allah, and indeed to Him we will return.
O Ever-Living, O Sustainer+
يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ أَسْتَغِيثُ O Ever-Living, O Sustainer, by Your mercy I seek help.

Struggling & Hardship

Nothing is easy except what You ease+
اللَّهُمَّ لَا سَهْلَ إِلَّا مَا جَعَلْتَهُ سَهْلًا، وَأَنْتَ تَجْعَلُ الْحَزْنَ إِذَا شِئْتَ سَهْلًا O Allah, nothing is easy except what You make easy, and You make the difficult easy if You will.
The dua of Nuh (AS) when overwhelmed+
رَبِّ إِنِّي مَغْلُوبٌ فَانْتَصِرْ My Lord, indeed I have been overpowered, so help me.
Asking for patience and firm footing+
رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَنَا Our Lord, pour upon us patience and make our steps firm.
He is the best Disposer of affairs+
حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.

Guidance

Guide us to the straight path+
اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ Guide us to the straight path.
Guide me among those You have guided+
اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنِي فِيمَنْ هَدَيْتَ O Allah, guide me among those You have guided.
Increase me in knowledge+
رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا My Lord, increase me in knowledge.
Do not let our hearts deviate+
رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحْمَةً Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself.

For Parents

As they raised me when I was small+
رَبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا My Lord, have mercy upon them as they raised me when I was small.
Forgiveness for myself and my parents+
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ Our Lord, forgive me and my parents and the believers on the Day the account is established.
Gratitude for their favor+
رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِي أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ الَّتِي أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَالِدَيَّ My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favor which You bestowed upon me and upon my parents.
For a parent who has passed away+
اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لَهُ وَارْحَمْهُ وَعَافِهِ وَاعْفُ عَنْهُ O Allah, forgive them, have mercy on them, keep them safe, and pardon them.

Before Sleeping

O Allah, in Your name I die and I live+
اللَّهُمَّ بِاسْمِكَ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا O Allah, in Your name I die and I live.
Protection through the night+
اللَّهُمَّ قِنِي عَذَابَكَ يَوْمَ تَبْعَثُ عِبَادَكَ O Allah, protect me from Your punishment on the Day You resurrect Your servants.

For Everyone

Good in this world and the next+
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
The dua of Musa (AS) before a hard task+
رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي My Lord, expand for me my chest and ease for me my task.
Asking for well-being+
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ O Allah, I ask You for well-being in this world and the Hereafter.
Accept this from us+

Seeking Forgiveness

A moment set apart — turning back is always met halfway.

Forgive me and accept my repentance+
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَتُبْ عَلَيَّ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ My Lord, forgive me and accept my repentance; indeed You are the Accepting of Repentance, the Merciful.
Sayyid al-Istighfar — the master of seeking forgiveness+
اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَىٰ عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ وَأَبُوءُ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ O Allah, You are my Lord, there is no deity but You. You created me and I am Your servant, and I hold to Your covenant as best I can. I acknowledge Your favor upon me, and I acknowledge my sin — so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You.
Seeking forgiveness from the Mighty+
أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ الَّذِي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ I seek forgiveness from Allah the Mighty, besides whom there is no deity, the Living, the Sustainer, and I turn to Him in repentance.
The dua of Adam and Hawwa (AS)+
رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers.

Dhikr

Morning

Dominion belongs to Allah this morning+
أَصْبَحْنَا وَأَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ We have entered the morning, and with it all dominion belongs to Allah, and all praise is for Allah.
By You we live, by You we die+
اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ النُّشُورُ O Allah, by You we enter the morning and by You we enter the evening; by You we live and by You we die, and to You is the resurrection.
Asking for a good day+
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا، وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, good provision, and accepted deeds.
Glory and praise, morning and evening+
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ Glory be to Allah, and praise be to Him — a light phrase repeated often through the morning.

Evening

Dominion belongs to Allah this evening+
أَمْسَيْنَا وَأَمْسَى الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ We have entered the evening, and with it all dominion belongs to Allah, and all praise is for Allah.
By You we return+
اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ O Allah, by You we enter the evening and by You we enter the morning; by You we live and by You we die, and to You is the return.
Refuge in the perfect words of Allah+
أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created.
Asking for the good of the night+
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ هَذِهِ اللَّيْلَةِ O Allah, I ask You for the good of this night.

Before You Sleep

Let the last thing you do tonight be quiet, not scattered. It's authentically narrated that the Prophet ﷺ would recite these closing two ayahs of Surah Al-Baqarah each night — they are said to be sufficient for the one who recites them.

Al-Baqarah, 2:285

آمَنَ الرَّسُولُ بِمَا أُنزِلَ إِلَيْهِ مِن رَّبِّهِ وَالْمُؤْمِنُونَ ۚ كُلٌّ آمَنَ بِاللَّهِ وَمَلَائِكَتِهِ وَكُتُبِهِ وَرُسُلِهِ لَا نُفَرِّقُ بَيْنَ أَحَدٍ مِّن رُّسُلِهِ ۚ وَقَالُوا سَمِعْنَا وَأَطَعْنَا ۖ غُفْرَانَكَ رَبَّنَا وَإِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيرُ

The Messenger believes in what has been sent down to him from his Lord, and so do the believers. Each one believes in Allah, His angels, His books, and His messengers — "We do not separate between any of His messengers." And they say: We hear and we obey. Grant us Your forgiveness, our Lord, and to You is the final return.

Al-Baqarah, 2:286

لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا ۚ لَهَا مَا كَسَبَتْ وَعَلَيْهَا مَا اكْتَسَبَتْ ۗ رَبَّنَا لَا تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِن نَّسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلَا تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لَا طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِ ۖ وَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا ۚ أَنتَ مَوْلَانَا فَانصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ

Allah does not burden a soul beyond what it can bear. It receives what it has earned, and is held to account for what it has done. Our Lord, do not take us to task if we forget or make a mistake. Our Lord, do not lay upon us a burden like the one You laid on those before us. Our Lord, do not burden us with more than we have strength to bear. Pardon us, forgive us, have mercy on us. You are our Protector, so grant us victory over the disbelieving people.

(a rendering of the meaning, not a formal translation)
